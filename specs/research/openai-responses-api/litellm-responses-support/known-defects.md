# Known defects in LiteLLM's chat-to-responses bridge (as of 1.102.0)

Each row: what happens, the evidence in 1.102.0 (code line in the installed wheel plus a fake-server observation), and the status in newer releases. Release status comes from (a) diffs of downloaded PyPI wheels and (b) BerriAI release notes, issues and PRs read on the web on 2026-10-30. Wheels checked: 1.102.4 (latest 1.102.x, 2026-10-08), 1.103.10, 1.104.8, 1.105.0, 1.105.7, 1.106.0, 1.106.1, 1.106.6 (latest stable on PyPI, 2026-10-28) and 1.108.0rc1 (2026-10-29). Kiln pins `litellm>=1.102.0,<1.103`.

File shorthand: `T` = `completion_extras/litellm_responses_transformation/transformation.py`.

## What "fixed in 1.103.5" would mean for Kiln

Defects 1, 2 and 12 are fixed only on release lines Kiln's pin excludes. The smallest bump that has all three is 1.103.5 (2026-10-17). Defect 11 needs 1.106.0. Defects 3 to 10 and 13 to 19 are present in every version checked, including 1.108.0rc1. Any design that keeps the bridge has to work around them in Kiln (pass `strict`, `store` and `include` explicitly; collect `reasoning_items` from the terminal chunk; ignore choice indexes on reasoning deltas; merge choices defensively). A native `aresponses()` path avoids 1-4, 6, 9-10 and 13 because Kiln would build the request and read the output items itself (see [native-responses-api.md](./native-responses-api.md)).
