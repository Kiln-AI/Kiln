# Plan Tiers and Response Targets

Three plan tiers appear in tickets: free, pro, and enterprise. They change response
targets and which channels are available. They do not change priority, and they do
not change whether a ticket is escalated.

## Free

Email support only. First response within three business days. No phone support and
no named contact. Free-tier customers are subject to the same escalation policy as
everyone else: a data exposure or a security report from a free account is escalated
exactly as an enterprise one is.

## Pro

Email and in-app chat. First response within one business day, and within four hours
for tickets marked urgent. Pro customers can request a callback for urgent tickets
but do not have a named contact.

## Enterprise

Email, chat, and a shared channel with a named account manager. First response within
four business hours, and within one hour for urgent tickets. Enterprise customers
have a quarterly review where recurring ticket themes are discussed.

## What the targets mean for triage

The response target is a commitment about the first reply, not about resolution. Do
not promise a resolution time in a first reply. Say what has been understood, what is
happening next, and who is doing it.

An enterprise ticket that is genuinely low priority stays low priority. The tier
shortens the clock on the first reply; it does not move the ticket up the queue ahead
of a higher-priority ticket from a smaller customer.
