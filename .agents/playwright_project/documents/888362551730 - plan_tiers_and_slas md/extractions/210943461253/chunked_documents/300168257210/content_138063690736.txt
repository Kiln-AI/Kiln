It is not escalated, and it does not change the ticket's priority.
The correct response to a missed target is to reply, not to reprioritise the queue
around the clock.

## Common misreadings

The target is per ticket, not per message. A customer who sends four follow-ups
before anyone replies is owed one first response, not four.

The target does not restart when a ticket is reassigned between teams. The clock runs
from when the customer first wrote in.

Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period, so a customer who
downgraded yesterday still has their previous tier's targets until their period ends.
Upgrades take effect immediately, including on tickets that are already open.

## Where the tiers are recorded

The customer_plan field on a triaged ticket carries the tier at the moment the ticket
was received. It is a snapshot, not a live lookup, so a ticket raised before an
upgrade keeps the old value even after the upgrade lands.
