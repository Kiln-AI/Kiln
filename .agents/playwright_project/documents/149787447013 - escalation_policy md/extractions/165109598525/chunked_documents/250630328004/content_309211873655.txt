**A customer mentions their lawyer in passing while complaining about downtime.**
Escalate. Threats of legal action are escalated whether or not they are serious,
because judging seriousness is exactly the thing a human should do.

**A customer asks us to delete all their data.** Escalate if they cite a law or a
regulator. An ordinary account-deletion request is not an escalation.

## What escalation is not

Escalation is not a priority level, and it is not a way to get a ticket answered
faster. A low-priority ticket can be escalated and stay low priority. An urgent
ticket can be answered automatically and never involve a human at all.

Escalation is also not a judgement about the customer. Nothing about the customer's
tone, plan tier, or history changes whether a ticket is escalated. The four
categories above are the whole test.

## After escalation

The reviewer records which of the four categories applied. Those records are reviewed
monthly, and a category that is firing constantly on tickets the reviewer then
releases unchanged is a sign the rule is drawn too widely, not a sign the reviewers
are being careless.
